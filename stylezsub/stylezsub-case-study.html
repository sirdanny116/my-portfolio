StylezSUB

A modern subscription management platform designed to help users discover, manage and track their subscriptions, payments and digital services in one simple experience.

My Role UI/UX Designer & Developer
Timeline 2026
Tools HTML • CSS • JavaScript
Platform Responsive Web App

Project Overview

StylezSUB is a subscription management concept created to make it easier for users to keep track of the services they pay for and discover new services that match their lifestyle.

The Problem

Managing multiple subscriptions can become confusing. Users may forget what services they are paying for, how much they spend each month, or where to manage their subscriptions.

The Goal

The goal was to create a simple and intuitive platform that brings subscriptions, payments, discovery and account management into one clean interface.

Key Features

✨

Discover

Explore entertainment, food, travel, fitness and gaming services.

🔄

Subscriptions

View and manage active subscriptions from one place.

💳

Wallet

Track balances, payment methods and transaction activity.

👤

Profile

Manage personal information, preferences and account settings.

Design Approach

The interface was designed around simplicity, clarity and easy navigation. Important information is grouped into focused sections so users can understand the interface quickly.

Clean Interface

A minimal layout with clear hierarchy, spacious cards and focused content.

Consistent Visual Language

Consistent typography, spacing, buttons, cards and purple accent colors create a unified experience.

Responsive Design

The application adapts to smaller screens with a mobile bottom navigation system.

User-Friendly Navigation

Home, Discover, Subscriptions, Wallet and Profile remain easy to access throughout the experience.

Mobile responsive experience

Challenges & Solutions

Mobile Responsiveness

Complex dashboard content needed to remain comfortable and easy to navigate on smaller screens. The interface was adapted with a mobile bottom navigation system and responsive card layouts.

Information Hierarchy

Content was grouped into clear sections and cards so users could quickly understand what they were seeing without feeling overwhelmed.

Horizontal Mobile Overflow

The Discover experience initially created unwanted horizontal movement on mobile. The responsive layout was refined so the content remains within the viewport while category controls retain their intended horizontal scrolling behavior.

The Final Result

StylezSUB evolved into a clean, responsive and functional subscription management experience. The project demonstrates my ability to combine UI/UX thinking with front-end development to create practical digital products.
